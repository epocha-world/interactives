Five human competencies that complement AI

The EPOCH framework is not primarily a leadership development model. It is a labour economics research framework developed at MIT Sloan Business School to measure which human work tasks are most resilient to AI substitution. The researchers - Isabella Loaiza and Roberto Rigobon - found that all EPOCH capability groups were associated with employment growth.
